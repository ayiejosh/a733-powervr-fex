# PRECISE LOCALISATION: float ALU is 5.13x slower, integer ALU is 1.12x

Built `vkalu`: the same 32-iteration loop as `vkheavy` but with **no transcendentals** — only float
`mul`/`add`/`min`/`max`/`fract`. Per-job durations, both drivers:

| shader | open | vendor | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| SFU-heavy (`vkheavy`: sin/cos/sqrt/normalize) | 858.4 ms | 357.2 ms | **2.40x** |
| **ALU-only (`vkalu`: float mul/add/min/max/fract)** | **660.9 ms** | **128.8 ms** | **5.13x** |
| compute (`cstp`: **integer** ALU) | 393.7 M inv/s | 441.9 M inv/s | **1.12x** |

## Pinned down

* **ALU-only is *worse* than SFU-heavy (5.13x vs 2.40x)** — the deficit is **not** transcendentals.
* **`vkalu` is float; `cstp` is integer. Float ALU is 5.13x down while integer ALU is 1.12x.** The driver's
  **FP32 arithmetic throughput is ~5x the vendor's; integer is at parity.**
* The vendor's own shape confirms it: **its ALU-only (128.8 ms) is 2.8x faster than its SFU-heavy
  (357.2 ms)**, while the open driver's ALU-only (660.9) is only 1.3x faster than its SFU-heavy (858.4) —
  the open driver's plain float ALU is disproportionately slow relative to its own SFU.

## Correction to the previous framing

**"Fragment ALU 2.4x slower" conflated two variables.** `vkheavy` is SFU-heavy (2.40x down); `vkalu` is
pure float ALU (5.13x down). **The larger effect is on plain float arithmetic, and the compute-vs-fragment
comparison was confounded by operation type** (the compute probe uses integer ALU).

## Where the problem lives

**FP32 arithmetic in the shader path.** Candidates, all in Mesa's scope:

1. **PCO's float codegen** — whether it emits native FP32 ops or a costlier form (packed/half-rate, or
   extra instructions per float op).
2. **The USC's float configuration** — `usc_f16sop_u8`, `usc_alu_roundingmode_rne` in the device info; a
   mode that halves/quarters FP32 rate gives exactly this.
3. The DOUTU `sample_rate`/task settings for float-heavy shaders (the sample-rate mode was measured null
   earlier, but on the *trivial* shader — worth re-checking on a float-heavy one).

`full_rate` was investigated and is the **transfer** path's sample-rate control
(`pvr_transfer_frag_store.c:408` → `ROGUE_PDSINST_DOUTU_SAMPLE_RATE_FULL`), not the render path's.

## Why this is the best result of the session

**Integer ALU 1.12x and float ALU 5.13x on the same GPU, same driver, same frame budget.** That is not
clock, memory, tiling, geometry, occupancy or scheduling — it is the **float execution path**, measurable
in one run, and a concrete code area rather than a platform mystery.
